Launch Search Portal

Transparency in the justice system is vital for public safety and family support. The official Sedgwick County Inmate Search provides real-time access to the adult detention facility roster, ensuring that victims, attorneys, and family members have accurate custody information.

Pro-Tip: When using the search tool, ensure you have the correct spelling of the individual's last name for the most accurate results.

Accessing Inmate Records Online

The digital lookup system serves as a bridge between the facility and the community. By visiting sedgwickinmatesearch.org, you can skip the long phone wait times and find answers instantly.

Data Available Through the Portal

  • Current Location: Specific housing assignments within the jail.
  • Booking Details: Time and date of entry into the facility.
  • Bond Status: Current bail amounts and type of bond required.
  • Case Information: Active charges and upcoming court appearances.

Communication & Family Support

Staying connected with an incarcerated individual is key to their rehabilitation. Use the Search Guide to find specific booking numbers required for:

1. Depositing Funds: Ensuring the inmate has access to commissary.
2. Scheduling Visits: Both in-person and video visitation options.
3. Mailing: Following the strict guidelines for physical correspondence.
